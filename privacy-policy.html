ZoomMeetingBook Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

ZoomMeetingBook is a personal meeting book for Zoom. This policy describes how the app handles information on your device.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Meeting titles, Zoom links, passcodes, notes, and schedules are stored locally on your device in an on-device database.

Recovery meeting search

When you search for recovery meetings, the app fetches public fellowship directories over the internet. We do not log or upload your search queries to our servers.

Backups

Export and encrypted backup features write files you control. Optional Google Drive auto-backup updates a file you choose in your Google Drive account. Backup passphrases are stored encrypted on your device only when you enable Drive backup.

Notifications

The app uses notifications only for meeting reminders and optional heads-up alerts you enable.

Third-party services

  • Zoom — joining meetings opens the official Zoom app or browser.
  • Google Play — in-app updates when installed from Google Play.
  • Google Drive — only when you explicitly link a backup file.
  • Fellowship directories — public meeting listings when you search recovery programs.

Analytics and ads

No third-party analytics SDKs. No ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository or contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
